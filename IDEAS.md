# Ideas

Anything not on ROADMAP.md, one line each with why. Reviewed at each gate, not acted on between them.

- Fishing boats that fight (harpoon skiff): in ROADMAP Milestone 3/4, kept here as the owner's original ask so it is not lost.
- A game-warden joke boss who confiscates the catch: Milestone 4 or later.
- Terrapins shouldn't be *sold*: commercial harvest of diamondback terrapins is banned or tightly limited along most of the Gulf. A "release" button that pays in something other than money (luck: the next catch is a variant; or the catch log's lore) would be truer. Owner's call; it touches the currency list.
- Sell-or-deploy needs somewhere for money to go. Partly answered 2026-10-12 (the critic's second review): with fish wearing out and the rod's job being rays at the point, selling puffers to buy the rod first is worth ~27 wins over never selling. Past the bed and the rod there is still nothing to buy; Milestone 3's boats, canals and chum are the sinks.
- Sending a wave early pays nothing now (the bonus was Kingdom Rush's). If the gate says the waiting drags, bank the unused build time toward the next build instead of paying money.
- Money piles up after the rod and the bed ($42-47 for the strong bot). The critic's other sink, a worm fed to a deployed fish to heal it, was not taken (a new verb; feeding wild fish isn't angling). Milestone 3's boats, canals and chum are the real sinks.
- The re-gate review's G (2026-10-12): past the rod, money had no use ($50-55 unspent; at the gate review bait at 8 and two worms on the road brought it to $21-22) and the worm bed still measures the same as never buying it at any price from 20 to 40. The critic suggested a heavy surf rod at ~100 (a third rod tier: reach the far side of the point, or a heavier line for bigger fish) or a cheaper bed. A third tier is Milestone 3 scope (ROADMAP: tiers 1-2 in the slice); owner's call. If the bed is kept, it wants a job packs can't do (worms between waves only it can supply, say), not a price.
- Selling and the rod are near-forced (never selling wins none in 500, no rod 0-2 in 100). The gate review found the fork inside it (sell the dawn ray toward the rod, or deploy it: within 6 wins of each other) and the first hint names it. If the gate wants the opening to be a choice rather than a line, a second road to the rays (rays catchable in numbers by the reeds at a lower rate, or a cheaper first rod that reaches the point's near side) would make "deploy now, rod later" a real alternative.
- A standing worm (a lure that doesn't spoil at the wave's end, at a price) came up in the re-gate review's lure findings; not taken: spoiling is what makes the lure a timed decision.
- Canals want a shore whose road runs inland (Milestone 3 measured them at nothing on Back Bay, where the road hugs the water and slots are plentiful). A Milestone 5 map with a dry stretch between two bays, or a pool that floods only when dug through, would make digging the kill zone the decision pillar 4 promises.
- A heavier trawl or a second net for the trawler (more chum, fewer fish) if the slick economy wants a dedicated bait boat later; the trawler now does both at once.
- Boats that can be told what to fish for (bait choice per boat: worms for rays and puffers, live shrimp for Milestone 4's redfish) instead of where only.
- The late board reads like a lawn row (the critic's Milestone 3 finding 10: one straight road with identical shooters two tiles off it). The shared ray stock thinned the row (28 rays at the finale to ~20, more puffers and slicks); Milestone 4's roster (an otter that takes rays out of the water, a heron that picks off puffers) is what would break it up.
- A late money sink (2026-10-12 re-measurement: the reference starts the finale with $128-140 and nothing to buy but bait). Candidates, each a design call: a second boat of a kind (pillar 5: scaling the fleet; the dock has room for more berths), a third rod tier (the heavy surf rod the re-gate review suggested), or Milestone 4's roster bought at the bait shop. Not taken here: no new tree without a DECISIONS entry naming the pillar.
- Terrapins earn their place again: over a run they measure as worth nothing once slicks hold bears and the ray row the raccoons. A job only a shell does (holding a raccoon line out of a gull flock's landing, or a bear's wading reach) would make them a choice; Milestone 4's otter, which takes rays, might do it by itself.
